# Team Walleye

## This is to document the website created for the project

This is to assist anglers who are interested in the study and if they recapture any of my study fish!

Watts Bar Reservoir is a popular recreational area, with anglers from all over the state to fish the mainstem Tennessee River!

In 2011, Tennessee Wildlife Resources Agency Region 3 started stocking Walleye (*sander vitreus*) because in the south, they tend to grow faster, live longer, and provide better year round angling opportunities! 

In partnership with UT and TWRA, my project is focusing on the broadscale movement patterns of Walleye in Watts Bar Reservoir! 


I'm a firm believer in making science accessible and understandable to the people we are serving. I am grateful for all anglers participating in the study and want to give them a deliverable on project updates and what's happening with the Walleye! They are stakeholders, too.
